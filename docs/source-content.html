FAQs about online medicines and healthcare services

What are online medicine services?

Online medicine services allow people to access medicine information, pharmacy support, and eligible prescription services through websites or digital platforms.

Are online medicine services safe in the UK?

They can be safe when you use legitimate, appropriately registered providers. Check the provider's registration with the relevant UK regulator and avoid websites selling prescription medicines without a valid prescription.

Can I buy prescription medicines online in the UK?

Yes, prescription medicines can be supplied online when the applicable legal requirements are met. Some medicines require a prescription from an authorised prescriber before they can be dispensed.

Can I get healthcare guidance online?

Yes, online services can provide general health information, medicine guidance, and information about available healthcare options. However, online information does not replace professional medical advice or diagnosis.

How can I check whether an online pharmacy is legitimate?

Check the provider's registration with the appropriate regulator, such as the General Pharmaceutical Council (GPhC) in Great Britain or the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (PSNI) in Northern Ireland.

Can online healthcare services help me understand medicine side effects?

Yes, reputable healthcare resources can explain common side effects, precautions, and interactions. Always read the patient information leaflet and consult a pharmacist or prescriber if you have concerns.

When should I seek medical help instead of using online healthcare services?

Seek urgent medical advice if your symptoms are worsening or concerning. In England, you can contact NHS 111 for urgent medical guidance; call 999 for a life-threatening emergency. Different NHS arrangements apply in Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.
